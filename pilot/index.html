Gesture pairs, pilot set

Two animated figures, one sentence. Which movement shows the meaning of the marked word better?

You will see 50 pairs of short clips. The two figures say the same sentence, without sound. One word is marked.

  1. Watch both figures and look closely at the hands and fingers. Many gestures are small.
  2. Click the figure whose movement shows the meaning of the marked word better. If neither does, or both do equally, choose that below the clips.

The clips play at half speed. An orange tick marks when the word is said, but the gesture can come a little before or after it. Movement that only keeps the rhythm of speech does not count.
